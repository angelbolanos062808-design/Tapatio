Our Performances

Explore the dances and traditions that make Grupo Tapatio Folklore what we are. Every performance is an opportunity to celebrate the culture we love.

PERFORMANCE 01

Son de la Negra

One of the most recognizable pieces of Mexican folklórico, this energetic performance celebrates the music, movement, and spirit of Jalisco.

Region: Jalisco, Mexico

Style: Traditional Folklórico

Jalisco

A celebration of the traditions and lively spirit of Jalisco. Our dancers bring together movement, music, and colorful costumes to create an unforgettable performance.

Region: Jalisco, Mexico

Style: Regional Folklórico
